About Us

Travel the Hilway Way

Hilway Travels is a travel agency based in Poblacion, Kalibo, Aklan, committed to creating smooth, reliable, and meaningful travel experiences for every client. Inspired by the Aklanon/Hiligaynon word "Hilway," which conveys a deep sense of calmness, serenity, orderliness, and harmony, our agency aims to make every journey feel easy, well-organized, and worry-free.

At Hilway Travels, we believe that travel should not be stressful or confusing. It should be exciting, comfortable, and memorable. That is why we focus on providing practical travel solutions with genuine customer care 🤝, helping individuals, families, groups, and business travelers plan their trips with confidence and peace of mind.

Rooted in the heart of Aklan, we are proud to serve our community and travelers beyond, offering dependable services that turn travel plans into seamless experiences.
