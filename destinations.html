DESTINATIONS

Handpicked tours crafted for every kind of traveler — from island escapes to world landmarks.

🏝️

Domestic Destinations

Discover the breathtaking beauty of the Philippines — crystal beaches, highland escapes, and vibrant culture await.

Bukidnon Domestic
🗓️ 4D / 3N 👫 Couples & Nature Lovers

A romantic highland escape with glamping at Taglucop Strawberry Hills, scenic RotyPeaks, Communal Ranch, Lake Apo, and the Monastery of Transfiguration.

Cebu & Bohol Domestic
🗓️ 7D / 6N 🌊 Adventure & Sightseeing

Kawasan Falls canyoneering, Oslob whale sharks, Chocolate Hills, Tarsier Sanctuary, and Seven Seas — the best of two iconic islands in one trip.

Siargao Domestic
🗓️ 3D / 2N 👥 Group of 4

Island-hopping to Naked Island, Daku, and Guyam, land tours to Cloud 9 and Magpupungko Rock Pools — pure island bliss for small squads.

La Union Domestic
🗓️ 3D / 2N 🏄 Beach & Surf Vibes

Surf lessons, pottery making, Nalvo Pebble Beach, Bahay na Bato, and the iconic Bato de Luna landmark — a full coastal escape for everyone.

✈️

International Destinations

From Asia's vibrant cities to the world's most iconic landmarks — expertly curated international tours await.

Singapore International
🗓️ 5D / 4N 🦁 4-Star Hotel

Merlion Park, Gardens by the Bay, Jewel Changi Airport, and Marina Bay Sands — the Lion City's best attractions with a professional tour guide.

Japan · Tokyo–Hakone International
🗓️ 5D / 4N 🌸 PAL Airfare Included

Shibuya Crossing, Meiji Jingu, Hakone Shrine, Ropeway, Pirate Ship cruise, Shibuya Sky, and a full day at Tokyo DisneySea — plus unlimited WiFi.

Japan · Osaka–Kyoto International
🗓️ 5D / 4N 🏯 Culture & Scenery

Ancient temples of Kyoto, the vibrant Dotonbori streets of Osaka, autumn landscapes, guided tours, pocket WiFi, and airfare all included.
