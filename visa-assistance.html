Visa Assistance

We guide you through every document — so you can focus on the journey ahead.

Visa Requirements by Destination

Select your destination country below and review the requirements. Our team is ready to assist you every step of the way.

Dubai 🇦🇪
Requirements
  • Valid Passport (min. 6 months from travel date)
  • Passport-Size Photo (plain background, professional)
  • Travel Details
  • Accommodation Details
  • Financial Proof (if required)
  • Travel Insurance (optional but recommended)
Processing time may vary. Contact us for latest updates.
Japan 🇯🇵
Requirements
  • Valid Passport
  • Visa Application Form
  • Recent Passport Photo (white background)
  • PSA Birth Certificate
  • PSA Marriage Certificate (if applicable)
  • Daily Itinerary (only provided after booking)
  • Hotel Booking Confirmation (confirmed clients only)
  • Bank Certificate & Bank Statement (3–6 months)
  • Certificate of Employment or Business Documents
  • ITR / Form 2316
  • Sponsor Documents (if applicable)
Documents submitted only after confirmed booking.
South Korea 🇰🇷
Requirements
  • Valid Passport
  • Korea Visa Application Form (typewritten)
  • One Recent Passport-Size Photo (white background)
  • Original Personal Bank Certificate
  • Data Privacy Consent Form
  • Original PSA Documents (if traveling with immediate family)
Application form must be typewritten, not handwritten.
Europe (Schengen) 🇪🇺
Requirements
  • Valid Passport
  • Duly Accomplished & Signed Application Form
  • Recent Passport-Size Photo (ICAO standards)
  • Supporting Documents
  • Fingerprints upon application submission
  • Additional documents may be requested by consulate
Biometrics (fingerprints) collected at submission.
United Kingdom 🇬🇧
Requirements
  • Valid Passport
  • Completed Online Visa Application
  • Visa Fee Payment
  • Biometrics Appointment (photo & fingerprints)
  • Proof of Finances (bank statements, payslips)
  • Proof of Employment or School Enrollment
  • Travel Itinerary and Accommodation Details
  • Evidence of Return to Philippines
  • Sponsor Supporting Documents (if applicable)
Application is completed online before your appointment.
Australia 🇦🇺
Requirements
  • Valid Passport
  • Completed Online Application Form
  • Recent Passport-Style Photo
  • Proof of Financial Capacity
  • Proof of Employment, Business, or Enrollment
  • Evidence of Travel History (old visas/stamps)
  • Travel Itinerary or Planned Activities
  • Form 54 (Family Composition Form)
  • Sponsor Documents (if sponsored by family/friends)
  • Certificate of Employment & Approved Leave (employees)
  • Certificate of Enrollment (students)
Form 54 required for family applications.
Canada 🇨🇦
Requirements
  • Application Form IMM5257 (completed, dated, signed)
  • Family Information Form IMM5645
  • Passport Photos — 2 per applicant (recent)
  • Valid Passport
  • Proof of Funds (6-month bank statement)
  • Purpose of Trip — Itinerary, bookings, or employer letter
  • 2 Passport-Size Photos (colored, white background)
  • Income Tax Return (clear copy)
  • Bank Certificate (original)
IMM5257 & IMM5645 are required government forms.
Turkey 🇹🇷
Requirements
  • Valid Passport
  • Recent Passport Photos (2 pcs, 2x2, white background, ≤6 months old)
  • Round-Trip Flight Booking
  • Hotel Reservation / Accommodation Details
  • Copy of Detailed Itinerary
  • Bank Certificate and Bank Statement
  • PSA Birth Certificate (original)
  • PSA Marriage Certificate (original, if married)
  • Employment Documents
  • Sponsor Documents (if applicable)
  • Parental Consent & School Documents (if under 18)
  • Valid US, UK, Schengen, or Ireland Visa (for e-Visa eligibility)
e-Visa available with valid US/UK/Schengen/Ireland visa.
Spain 🇪🇸
Requirements
  • Completed Schengen Visa Application Form
  • Valid Passport
  • Recent Passport-Size Photo
  • Cover Letter
  • Round-Trip Flight Reservation
  • Hotel Booking / Accommodation Proof
  • Travel Itinerary
  • Travel Medical Insurance
  • Proof of Financial Capacity
  • Proof of Employment or Source of Income
  • For Minors: Birth Certificate, Parent/Guardian Consent, Copies of Parents' Passports
Spain requires medical travel insurance coverage.
China 🇨🇳
Requirements
  • Valid Passport
  • Visa Application Form (printed and signed)
  • Recent Visa Photo
  • Photocopy of Passport Bio Page
  • Proof of Travel (roundtrip flight reservation)
  • Proof of Accommodation
  • Travel Itinerary
  • Proof of Financial Capacity (bank certificate & statements 3–6 months)
  • Employment or Business Documents
  • Supporting Documents
Application form must be printed and hand-signed.
USA 🇺🇸
Requirements
  • Valid Passport
  • DS-160 Online Application Form
  • Visa Appointment Confirmation
  • Visa Fee Payment Receipt
  • Recent U.S. Visa Photo (digital copy for DS-160)
  • Proof of Strong Ties to Home Country
  • Proof of Financial Capacity
  • Travel Purpose Documents
  • Supporting Civil Documents
  • Personal Interview at the U.S. Embassy
Personal interview required at the U.S. Embassy.
